# Γ_{3⊕3} (equal-sign pairs in S₃ × S₃) is not 2-generated

**Classification:** First formal proof of a known result  
**Area:** Group theory  
**Source problem:** arXiv:2605.12342  
**Date:** 2026-10-17

## Result

Let Γ = Γ_{3⊕3} = {(σ₁, σ₂) ∈ S₃ × S₃ : sgn σ₁ = sgn σ₂}. In the formal statement Γ is the kernel of (σ₁,σ₂) ↦ sgn σ₁ · sgn σ₂⁻¹. For all h₁, h₂ ∈ Γ, the subgroup of Γ generated by {h₁, h₂} is not all of Γ. So Γ is not 2-generated, which is the rank-3 claim Fernandes records in arXiv:2605.12342.

## Notes

V. H. Fernandes, "Groups of permutations that are even on maximal proper subsets, and related monoids", arXiv:2605.12342 (2026). It states, as a GAP computation, that Γ3⊕3, Γ4⊕3 and Γ4⊕4 are not 2-generated, so rank(Γ3⊕3) = 3, and gives Conjecture 1 for all other (m, n). It also follows from elementary group theory: Γ3⊕3 is the generalized dihedral group Dih(C3×C3), and any 2-generated subgroup of it has order at most 9. Existing Lean work: K. Kitamura, github.com/KitaKen1/fernandes-conjecture-1-lean, proves only the main conjecture_1 (the non-exceptional cases are 2-generated), not the rank-3 exceptions.
